Important

Electrical & Plumbing Safety

Some electrical and plumbing work requires appropriate qualifications, registration, inspection or compliance documentation.

Electrical work

Do not attempt dangerous electrical repairs yourself. Switch off power where appropriate and seek professional assistance for electrical faults.


Plumbing work

If you have a serious leak or burst pipe, isolate the water supply where possible and request assistance to reduce potential property damage.


Compliance

Where a Certificate of Compliance or other regulated sign-off is required, this should be handled by an appropriately authorised professional.
